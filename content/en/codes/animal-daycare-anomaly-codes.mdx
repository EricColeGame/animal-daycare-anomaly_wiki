## Advanced Tips for Surviving the Night Shift

Finding impostors requires a methodical routine. You cannot simply guess who belongs; making an incorrect call can terrorize genuine children or invite a deadly creature straight into the playroom.

### 1. Master the Camera Rotation
Never spend more than five seconds staring at a single camera feed. Anomalies often exploit blind spots when caretakers tunnel-vision on the front entrance. Make a quick, sweeping check of the meal room, the dormitory hallway, and the exterior perimeter fences.

### 2. Verify Every Badge Detail
Compare arrival cards against the master ledger kept on your reception desk. Look closely at registration dates, stamped logos, and listed species. A single misspelled word or an inverted crest indicates an impostor attempting to bluff their way inside.

### 3. Keep Stress Levels Manageable
Genuine children panic if alarm klaxons blare too long or if food runs short. High panic meters attract nocturnal anomalies and make mini-games harder to complete. Use your code-funded food kits to maintain steady morale through the midnight hours.

### 4. Coordinate with Your Co-Workers
In multiplayer lobbies, divide responsibilities clearly. One caretaker should man the security monitors and verify badges, while the second roams the interior to soothe children and deliver prepared meals. Clear communication over voice or text chat minimizes confusion during surprise blackouts.
