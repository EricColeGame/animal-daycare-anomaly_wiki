## How to Spot Impostors: Anomaly Classification

Spotting fakes requires a keen eye. Based on community reports and player experiences, anomalies typically manifest across four distinct categories: physical deformities, sensory/camera glitches, behavioral inconsistencies, and temporal shifts.

### Common Anomaly Types and Warning Signs

| Anomaly Class | Visual or Audio Indicator | Threat Level | Correct Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anatomical Glitch** | Extra fingers/toes, wrong eye color, backwards feet | High | Immediate Denial |
| **Texture Drift** | Blurry fur patterns, misaligned mouth textures | Medium | Verify with handheld camera zoom |
| **Behavioral Lag** | Staring unblinkingly, delayed response to prompts | High | Deny entry; notify co-op partner |
| **Audio Distortion** | Robotic vocal tone, distorted background noise | Critical | Deny immediately; do not open inner door |

Use this step-by-step checklist whenever an arrival rings the bell:

1. **Check the Face:** Look directly at the eyes and snout. Are the pupils shaped correctly for the species?
2. **Examine the Limbs:** Ensure there are no extra digits, inverted joints, or clipping limbs.
3. **Switch Camera Angles:** Use your secondary security camera monitors to check for hidden shadows or unnatural reflections not visible from the front window.
4. **Listen to Dialogue:** If their speech pattern stutters or carries an unnatural echo, reject the visitor immediately.

| Room | Potential Danger | Defense Tool | Survival Procedure |

| **Front Vestibule** | Aggressive late-night arrival | Intercom Rejection | Do not unlock; sound alarm to repel entity |

### Step-by-Step Night Routine
1. Lock all exterior access doors at the stroke of nightfall.
2. Conduct a quick roll call in the sleeping quarters. Every animal must match their intake card.
3. Monitor hallway security feeds every 15–20 seconds.
4. If an unverified entity is spotted wandering the halls, lock down the adjacent sector and wait for them to retreat.

## Animal Daycare Anomaly Walkthrough: Pro Tips and Common Pitfalls

To finish your shift with a perfect rating, keep these advanced gameplay recommendations in mind:

- **Do Not Rely Solely on Lighting:** Some anomalies only reveal their true form in darkness or when viewed through camera night-vision filters.
- **Audio Cues Never Lie:** If you hear wet footsteps or electrical static while speaking to a visitor, they are an impostor regardless of how normal they look.
- **Manage Your Battery:** Keep your flashlight powered. Staring into the dark without a light source drains your character's sanity and distorts your vision, causing false alarms.
- **Memorize Species Baselines:** Learn the default ear lengths, tail shapes, and coloring of standard animal types so subtle changes jump out instantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most reliable way to spot an anomaly in the game?
The most reliable method outlined in this **animal daycare anomaly walkthrough** is cross-referencing direct visual inspection with secondary camera views. Impostors often appear normal to your bare eyes but cast distorted shadows or exhibit visual artifacts on security camera monitors.

### Can you replay shifts if an anomaly breaches the daycare?
Yes. If an anomaly slips through and triggers a failure state, you can restart your shift. Each run randomizes certain visitors, so memorize the anomaly rules rather than specific character names.

### Is playing solo harder than co-op?
Playing solo requires strict time management because you must juggle cooking, inspecting, and monitoring feeds by yourself. Co-op distributes these responsibilities, making room inspections and anomaly identification significantly easier.

### Does turning off lights help detect impostors?
In certain rooms, flipping off room lights and activating blacklight or infrared camera modes will reveal hidden skin markings or glowing eyes that remain invisible in regular daylight.
