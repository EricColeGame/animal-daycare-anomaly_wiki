## Visitor Intake: How to Spot Impostors and Anomalies

The front desk is your first line of defense. When a visitor approaches the entry gate, you must conduct a thorough physical and behavioral assessment before opening the security doors. Impostors mimic regular animal kids, but their biological and behavioral mimicry is rarely flawless.

### Physical Red Flags
Examine anatomical features meticulously. Regular visitors have uniform textures, symmetrical features, and correct limb counts. Impostors frequently display subtle biological glitches:
- Extra fingers, reversed paws, or mismatched claws.
- Unblinking eyes, dilated or missing pupils, and abnormal sclera colors.
- Inconsistent tail movement or tails detached from the body mesh.
- Subtle shifts in height or posture when unobserved.

### Behavioral Clues
Watch how the visitor reacts while waiting outside. Innocent children show playful idle animations, ask normal questions, or express hunger. Impostors may linger completely motionless, repeat glitched audio lines, or stand at bizarre angles facing away from the inspection camera.

| Anomaly Category | Common Visual Tell | Verification Action | Recommended Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Facial Distortion** | Stretched smile, missing nose, mismatched eyes | Use intake camera zoom | Immediate Entry Denial |
| **Limb Mutation** | Extra joints, elongated arms, reversed paws | Check baseline visitor chart | Lock security barrier |
| **Texture Glitch** | Discolored fur patches, flickering skin | Toggle UV / night inspection light | Trigger silent alarm |
| **Audio Anomaly** | Backwards voice lines, heavy breathing | Listen closely via intercom | Reject and dispatch patrol |

Whenever you doubt an incoming child's authenticity during your **animal daycare anomaly playthrough**, rely on strict elimination. Admitting one single anomaly can sabotage your entire run, triggering chaos inside the playroom.

## Daycare Maintenance: Cooking, Mini-Games, and Morale

Keeping legitimate animal guests happy is not just flavor; it directly influences your survival. As stress builds among the children, their distress sounds create auditory clutter that masks incoming monsters, while their panic can even summon darker anomalies into the playrooms.

### Meal Preparation
During the lunch phase, food orders appear on the kitchen ticket rail. You must prepare burgers, slice snacks, and distribute drinks promptly. Burnt or incorrect meals spike agitation meters rapidly.

1. Pick up raw ingredients from the pantry.
2. Cook patties on the grill until the timer icon turns green.
3. Assemble the meal on clean trays at the prep counter.
4. Deliver directly to the designated seating tables.

| Station | Task Type | Ideal Completion Window | Failure Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kitchen Grill** | Patty grilling & beverage pouring | Under 30 seconds per ticket | Lowers daycare calm meter |
| **Playground** | Ball-toss and rhythm mini-games | 45 seconds per session | Children wander into restricted zones |
| **Rest Area** | Calming story interaction | Under 60 seconds | Heightened ambient noise at night |

### Managing the Calm Meter
The facility Calm Meter represents overall psychological stability. Community reports suggest that keeping this bar above 70% significantly reduces the spawn rate of aggressive hallway stalkers. Use mini-games—such as basketball toss or rhythm toys—to calm crying visitors whenever an incident occurs.

## Nighttime Survival: Power Management and Entity Defense

The final stretch of the shift tests your multitasking limits. During the night phase, your building experiences power fluctuations, forcing you to budget electricity between door locks, surveillance monitors, and defensive floodlights.

### Power Management Strategy
Power drains continuously when security systems are active. Leaving cameras on or keeping motorized blast doors sealed drains your battery within minutes, leaving you completely defenseless in total darkness.

- **Door Usage:** Only close blast doors when an entity is physically verified in the adjacent corridor.
- **Camera Discipline:** Pulse camera feeds for 3–5 seconds per room rather than watching a single screen constantly.
- **Flashlight Conservation:** Avoid leaving your handheld light on; listen for audio cues like footsteps or tapping first.

| Threat Entity | Detection Method | Behavioral Pattern | Countermeasure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Mimic** | Cam 01 / Cam 06 | Poses as an abandoned child outside doors | Check UV light; do NOT unlock door |
| **Vent Crawler** | Clattering vent audio | Crawls toward office via overhead ducts | Seal office ceiling vent until thuds stop |
| **Shadow Lurker** | Dim flickering room lights | Disables hallway cameras sequentially | Pulse corridor spotlight to repel |
| **Static Specter** | TV monitor snow & glitching | Corrupts system tablets and UI | Reboot terminal via breaker switch |

In community reports, players note that the Vent Crawler produces distinct metallic clicks three seconds before striking. Training your ears to recognize spatial audio cues is crucial for finishing an **animal daycare anomaly playthrough** without succumbing to unexpected jump scares.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if an anomaly bypasses the intake gate?
If an impostor slips past your inspection booth, avoid panicking. Immediately pull up your security tablet, identify which room the entity entered, and trigger the emergency quarantine shutter for that wing. You must isolate the corrupted room to prevent the creature from terrorizing genuine visitors or reaching your main office.

### How do I balance cooking meals and checking security cameras?
Prepare basic ingredients ahead of time during quiet morning lulls. Keep patty stacks ready on the prep table so you only need to initiate the cooking timer when tickets appear. Use the cooking duration window to quickly flip through your camera feeds before returning to plate the food.

### Can anomalies disguise themselves as regular furniture?
Yes. During the nighttime phase, environmental anomalies frequently manifest as duplicated chairs, floating toys, or inverted wall clocks. While these do not immediately attack, failing to log or report them through your security terminal drains overall facility stability and can summon hostile entities.

### What is the best strategy for a deathless animal daycare anomaly playthrough?
The most reliable strategy for a deathless **animal daycare anomaly playthrough** is conservative power usage paired with strict intake screening. Never open the gate on borderline cases, memorize room geometry during the daytime, and rely on directional audio to locate vent threats before they enter your security office.
