## Core Mechanics That Influence Your Ending

Your primary duty during the day shift revolves around standard childcare operations: preparing meals, engaging children in mini-games, and monitoring stress levels. However, as documented on the [Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com), psychological horror titles challenge players by hiding supernatural threats behind mundane routines. At the reception desk, you must examine every newcomer, checking visual details, behavior, and physical quirks to determine if an entrant is a normal creature or a lethal mimic.

The choices you make accumulate hidden metrics throughout your play session. These metrics dictate the nighttime events and ultimately settle your storyline.

| Core Metric | How It Is Tracked | Primary Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inspection Accuracy** | Correctly admitting real visitors and rejecting impostors | Dictates whether anomalies infiltrate the building before lockup |

### 1. The Overrun Ending (Worst Ending)
This outcome occurs when players fail to identify multiple anomalies during the day shift. By letting two or more mimics slip past security, the nighttime lockdown collapses completely. Once the lights shut off, the mimics shed their disguise, leaving the caretaker cornered in the security booth. Community reports suggest this is the most common ending for beginners who rush through the front door camera inspections.

### 2. The Fired / Negligence Ending (Bad Ending)
You can successfully keep impostors out of the building and still fail. If you reject real animals due to paranoia or neglect basic duties—such as failing mini-games and leaving meals uncooked—the daycare inspection bureau terminates your employment at 6:00 AM. While you physically survive, your career is ruined, and the children remain unprotected.

### 3. The Shift Survivor Ending (Standard Good Ending)
To reach this finish, you must maintain an inspection accuracy of at least 80% and keep the children's panic gauge in the green zone. During the night, you only deal with external attempts to breach the facility. By rotating through surveillance feeds and locking security bulkheads when movement is detected, you survive until dawn and earn standard employee wages.

### 4. The Perfect Caretaker Ending (Best Ending)
Achieving this resolution requires a flawless run. Every visitor must be categorized with 100% precision, all meals served on time, and every nighttime disturbance countered without structural damage. The credits roll with commendations, showcasing all the safe children returning to their genuine parents.

### 5. The Classified Anomaly Ending (Secret/True Ending)
Reaching this obscure branch requires players to look beyond daily tasks. During the afternoon shift, players must uncover hidden anomalies inside the staff-only supply closets and piece together security logs. This triggers a confrontation with the facility’s director during the midnight lockdown, unveiling the true reason the daycare attracts these entities.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Get the Best Endings

Securing the top-tier **animal daycare anomaly endings** demands sharp observational skills and strict time management. Follow this structured roadmap to keep your facility safe.

### Phase 1: Mastering Reception and Anomaly Detection
When an animal approaches the front desk, resist the urge to stamp their admission pass immediately. Use your monitor to scan their full model:
- **Facial Features:** Look for unnatural blinking patterns, misplaced pupils, or symmetrical teeth that do not match the animal species.
- **Limbs and Movement:** Real children walk naturally; mimics often feature stiff limb animation or hovering models.
- **Paperwork Verification:** Cross-reference the name and species listed on the entrance clipboard with the creature standing in front of you.

```
       [ Visitor Approaches Reception Desk ]

        Check physical traits against file
                         │
             ┌───────────┴───────────┐
     [ Irregularity Detected ]   [ Matches File Perfectly ]
                 │                           │
          Hit Reject Button           Hit Admit Button
                 │                           │
         Turn Away Threat            Feed & Play Mini-Games
```

### Phase 2: Balancing Daycare Management
Once legitimate guests are admitted into the main playroom, you must manage their comfort levels to prevent panic:
1. **Prepare Meals:** Head to the kitchen station and match ingredients quickly to feed hungry children before their timers expire.
2. **Engage in Activities:** Play mini-games to burn off restless energy and keep stress levels manageable.
3. **Scan the Playroom:** Even after admission, observe how visitors interact with toys. A mimic that somehow slipped through will refuse to play or will stare directly into surveillance cameras.

### Phase 3: Surviving the Night Shift
The final hurdle to unlocking favorable **animal daycare anomaly endings** is the midnight phase. 

| Time Interval | Primary Objective | Threat Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **12:00 AM – 2:00 AM** | Power grid stabilization and door checks | Low |
| **2:00 AM – 4:00 AM** | Hallway audio cues; active mimic perimeter checks | Moderate |
| **4:00 AM – 6:00 AM** | High-aggression breach attempts; rapid camera swapping | Severe |

Conserve facility generator power by only closing security blast doors when an entity is physically present at the threshold. Leaving doors shut continuously drains battery reserves, leaving you defenseless during the brutal 5:00 AM rush.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you achieve all animal daycare anomaly endings in multiplayer?
Yes, multiplayer sessions support every ending branch. However, player reports indicate that unlocking the Perfect Caretaker and Secret endings is harder in co-op because a single mistake by any teammate can invalidate an admission check or deplete vital generator power.

### What is the telltale sign of an anomaly at the front gate?
The most reliable sign is an anatomical mismatch. Mimics often copy fur colors correctly but fail to replicate ear shapes, hand-drawn admission forms, or natural eye-tracking behavior. Always inspect the visitor's reflection in the lobby glass if camera angles are unclear.

### Why did I get the Negligence Ending even though no impostors entered?
The Negligence Ending occurs when your caretaking duties fall below minimum thresholds. If you ignore hungry children or fail mini-games, the daycare's happiness meter drops into critical status, resulting in immediate termination regardless of security accuracy.

### Do all animal daycare anomaly endings grant achievements or badges?
Yes, each distinct ending awards an exclusive in-game badge and player profile title. Earning the secret and flawless endings also unlocks custom cosmetic gear for your caretaker avatar to use in future runs.
