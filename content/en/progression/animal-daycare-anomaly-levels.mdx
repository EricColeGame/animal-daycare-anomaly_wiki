## Daily Schedule Breakdown and Task Priorities

To conquer the tougher stages, you must optimize your daily workflow. Every in-game day is partitioned into three distinct segments, each featuring unique hazards and chores that directly influence your end-of-shift evaluation.

### Morning Intake and Identity Verification

The morning begins with arrivals at the security desk. Every child seeking entry must undergo visual and behavioral clearance. Look out for unnatural limb proportions, upside-down clothing, distorted eye textures, or nonsensical dialogue prompts. Letting a single fake through will compromise the rest of your shift.

### Afternoon Care and Happiness Maintenance

Once everyone is admitted, your job transitions to daycare maintenance:
- **Meal Preparation:** Cook and assemble custom food orders in the kitchen. Neglecting hunger leads to tantrums that obscure noise cues.
- **Activity Time:** Play mini-games such as ball toss or toy sorting to burn off excess energy.
- **Emotional Regulation:** High distress levels make children vulnerable to psychological mimicry, clouding your ability to pinpoint true threats.

### Nighttime Lockdown

When the sun sets, the building switches to emergency power. This is where progression truly challenges your nerves. Any entity that slipped past your morning checks will roam halls, sabotage electrical breakers, and attempt to corner isolated survivors.

## Pro Tips for Solo and Co-op Play

Whether tackling the shifts alone or with a full crew, executing disciplined habits will keep your death count low:

1. **Establish a Headcount Routine:** Always verify the total number of approved children every time you move between play areas. If the counter reads higher than admitted, an intruder is present.
2. **Prioritize Kitchen Cleanliness:** Grease fires waste vital time during afternoon hours. Clean cookers immediately after serving meals.
3. **Save Generator Power:** Do not leave hallway floodlights burning during early evening hours. Conserve battery life for midnight surges.
4. **Communicate Anomalies Clearly:** In co-op mode, use descriptive callouts (e.g., "Bunny in Room B has inverted feet") rather than generic panic shouts to ensure fast responses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I let an impostor into the daycare?
If an anomaly bypasses the front inspection, it blends in until nighttime. Once lights go out, the creature will hunt children and staff, causing severe panic, system breakdowns, and often ending the shift in defeat.

### Can you replay earlier animal daycare anomaly levels to farm currency?
Yes, players can revisit cleared shifts from the lobby stage selector. This is an effective way to accumulate upgrade tokens, practice spotting high-tier mimics, and test newly acquired tools without risking difficult survival streaks.

### Does playing in co-op increase the number of anomalies?
Multiplayer sessions scale the number of daily visitors and chores to match the team size, which indirectly increases the frequency of anomaly spawns throughout the shift.

### How do I identify audio anomalies in late-game animal daycare anomaly levels?
Listen carefully to repeated voice lines during intake. Real visitors use normal vocal pitches and audible footsteps, whereas high-level entities often exhibit hollow reverberation, backward vocal tracks, or completely silent walking animations.
