### Intake Screener (The Gatekeeper)

The Screener stands at the facility threshold. Every creature knocking at the door must be scrutinized before entry is granted. You are looking for minor inconsistencies: asymmetrical features, abnormal limb counts, unblinking eyes, or conflicting paperwork. If the Screener lets an anomaly slip through the front entrance, the entire daycare faces an uphill battle.

### Activity Caretaker (The Stabilizer)

Once inside, genuine children require constant engagement. Caretakers handle cooking mechanics, run mini-games, and calm nervous children down before panic levels peak. Hungry or distressed children generate excess noise, masking the subtle audio cues emitted by roaming impostors.

### Surveillance Monitor (The Eye)

Tucked inside the security office, this player cycles through closed-circuit camera feeds. The Monitor watches for erratic movements, sudden disappearances from the play area, and unauthorized wanderers heading toward utility closets or restricted rooms.

### Nighttime Sentinel (The Enforcer)

When lights switch off and the facility enters lockdown, the Sentinel ensures doors remain locked and perimeter lights stay powered. When unexpected events trigger during midnight hours, Sentinels act as the first line of defense to quarantine threats.

## Spotting Anomalies: Inspection Checklists

Impostors rely on team distraction to slip through inspection. Whether you play the Screener or step in as a roving Sentinel, keeping a disciplined inspection protocol is essential. 

```
         INSPECTION CHECKPOINT
               [ Entrance ]
                    |

      |                           |

      |                           |
      +-------------+-------------+
                    |
          [ Verdict Decision ]
           /                 \
    (Admit Child)      (Trigger Alarm)
```

Player experience shows that anomalies rarely mirror authentic guests perfectly. Subtle visual and behavioral discrepancies give them away:

| Inspection Point | Normal Child Indicator | Anomaly / Impostor Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eyes & Gaze** | Natural blinking, responsive tracking | Fixed forward gaze, misaligned pupils |
| **Anatomy & Limbs** | Standard paws/hands, fluid movement | Extra digits, twitching joints, clipping mesh |
| **Voice & Speech** | Normal pitch, typical playful phrases | Garbled audio, delayed audio, flat tone |
| **Meal Response** | Consumes food, happiness bar rises | Ignores plate, reaction animation fails |
| **Playpen Behavior**| Interacts with toys and other kids | Loiters in corners, faces away from light |

When suspicious traits appear, never hesitate. Consult your security monitor teammate to verify whether the visitor's profile matches central records before granting admission.

### Thriving in Co-op Play

In co-op lobbies, communication protocols separate winning squads from failed shifts. Designate one player as the dedicated Screener at all times. If the Screener spots an ambiguous guest, the Monitor should instantly pull up camera records while the Caretaker keeps the main playpen quiet. Preventing cross-talk and establishing concise verbal cues ("Check Door B", "Unusual Guest at Window") keeps panic low.

### Overcoming Solo Play

When tackling solitary shifts, structure your workflow into 45-second micro-loops. Inspect one arriving guest, run directly to the kitchen to prep a plate, deliver the meal to the playpen, check two camera feeds on your way back, and repeat. Do not linger on security cameras; stationary players leave the front door completely exposed.

## Advanced Tips for Surviving the Night Phase

Surviving the daytime shift merely sets the stage for the true test: the night phase. Once dusk arrives, unadmitted entities stalk the perimeter, power boxes flicker, and internal temperatures drop. How your team executes its assigned **animal daycare anomaly roles** during these dark hours determines whether everyone clocks out safely the following morning.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                NIGHTFALL DEFENSE STRATEGY                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                             |
|  1. Lockdown: Seal all non-essential hallway doors          |
|  2. Power Management: Rotate generator usage to avoid overload |
|  3. Audio Focus: Listen for floorboard creaks & scratching  |
|  4. Containment: Corner anomalies with high-beam flashlights|
|                                                             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

Apply these community-tested survival tips during nighttime events:

- **Conserve Flashlight Batteries:** Flick your flashlight on for brief half-second pulses to navigate corridors. Leaving your beam active alerts wandering impostors to your exact coordinates.
- **Listen for Audio Tells:** Authentic children sleep quietly. Heavy breathing, dragging footsteps, or sudden metallic tapping against duct grates reveal an entity prowling nearby.
- **Keep Emergency Generators Fueled:** If main breaker panels pop, security cameras drop offline instantly. Have your Sentinel stand near backup switches before 2:00 AM strikes.
- **Isolate Suspicious Arrivals Promptly:** If a visitor slipped inside despite questionable traits earlier, isolate them in an individual resting quarters away from regular children.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the main animal daycare anomaly roles for beginner teams?
The fundamental roles are the Intake Screener, Activity Caretaker, Surveillance Monitor, and Nighttime Sentinel. Beginners should start with a Caretaker or Monitor role to observe gameplay loops and anomaly patterns safely before tackling high-pressure screening duties.

### Can you reassign animal daycare anomaly roles in the middle of a shift?
Yes. Swapping roles is often necessary if a teammate is overwhelmed, low on resources, or needs to assist with sudden night-phase hazards. Clear team communication is vital whenever players rotate stations.

### How do I identify impostors quickly without slowing down intake?
Focus on the face and limbs first. Inconsistencies in eye alignment, abnormal blinking animations, and odd limb movement are the most reliable indicators of an impostor. Compare paperwork details with real-time camera records to verify suspicious guests quickly.

### Are animal daycare anomaly roles mandatory when playing solo?
In single-player mode, you cannot assign separate roles; instead, you must juggle all responsibilities on your own. Success in solo play relies on creating a fast routine cycling between inspection, kitchen prep, and brief surveillance checks.
