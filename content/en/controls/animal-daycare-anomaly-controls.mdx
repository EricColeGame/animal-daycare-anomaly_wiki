## Troubleshooting Common Control Issues

Players occasionally report input latency or unresponsive keys during high-stress encounters. Use this checklist to solve common input problems quickly:

* **Stuck in Inspection Screen:** If your mouse cursor detaches from the UI or remains trapped during a checklist screen, tap **Esc** twice or hit **Tab** to force the interaction layer to reset.
* **Gamepad Inverted Axes:** Check the controller sub-menu in game options. Certain analog stick deadzones default to 0%, which can cause slow camera drift that ruins scan accuracy. Increase your stick deadzone to roughly 8%–12%.
* **Unresponsive Sprint:** Stamina depletion prevents sprinting. If pressing **Left Shift** produces no speed boost, watch your stamina gauge at the bottom-left before attempting to run past roaming entities.
* **Interact Key Delay:** When operating kitchen tools or breaker boxes, ensure you are holding the interact key (**E**) continuously rather than tapping it, as partial inputs will reset the completion circle.

Knowing your layout thoroughly removes mechanical friction so you can focus entirely on deducing which visitors are genuine cuddly animals and which are sinister anomalies waiting to disrupt your shift.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the primary animal daycare anomaly controls for identifying an impostor?
To detect an anomaly, approach the visitor at the security gate and hold **Tab** (or **LB / L1** on controller) to activate your inspection scanner. Use your **Scroll Wheel** or **Right Stick** to zoom in on specific features like eyes, paws, and ears. Once you compare their physical appearance with the daily manifest, press **Z** to admit them or **X** to deny entry and quarantine the subject.

### Can I remap the animal daycare anomaly controls on console?
Most console versions feature preset controller layouts rather than fully modular remapping. However, you can select between several accessibility schemes (such as Alternate Bumper Layouts) in the system menu, or utilize your console's native accessibility settings (PlayStation Accessibility Menu or Xbox Accessories App) to swap button inputs directly.

### Why is my flashlight not turning on during the night shift?
Your flashlight binds directly to the **F** key on PC or **D-Pad Up** on controllers. If it fails to illuminate, your battery level is likely empty. You must walk to the kitchen or utility closet and interact (**E**) with the battery recharging cradle to restore your portable light.

### Is there a push-to-talk key in co-op multiplayer?
Yes. When playing in co-op multiplayer, the default push-to-talk button is bound to **V** on keyboard and mouse setups. You can switch this to Open Mic or customize the voice activation threshold within the in-game audio options menu.
