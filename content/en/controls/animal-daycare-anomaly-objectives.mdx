## Mastering the Surveillance System and Camera Feeds

Your surveillance station serves as your defensive command post. The daycare facility is outfitted with closed-circuit cameras covering corridors, the yard, utility rooms, and dormitories.

Checking cameras randomly will waste valuable power and leave your physical position exposed. Establish a consistent sweep pattern:

1. **Camera 01 (Front Gates)**: Scan for approaching entities before they knock.
2. **Camera 03 (Playroom & Common Area)**: Verify that children are playing safely and no rogue figures stand in the corners.
3. **Camera 05 (Kitchen & Storage)**: Check that supplies are intact and utility doors remain shut.
4. **Camera 07 (Ventilation & Back Alley)**: Catch high-threat anomalies attempting entry through service ducts.

```
Standard Camera Sweep Order:
CAM 01 (Perimeter) ➔ CAM 03 (Indoor Commons) ➔ CAM 05 (Supply) ➔ CAM 07 (Service Access)
Repeat every 20–30 seconds.
```

## Crucial Tips for Solo vs. Cooperative Play

Whether tackling duties alone or coordinating with friends, teamwork drastically changes your task management approach.

### Solo Strategy
- **Prioritize Multitasking**: Carry a meal tray while walking to check perimeter doors.
- **Limit Camera Time**: Do not remain glued to screens for more than 5 seconds at a stretch.
- **Listen for Audio Cues**: Sound design gives away intruder positions long before they reach your office.

### Cooperative Multiplayer Strategy
- **Designate Roles**: Assign one player as the dedicated Security Monitor/Cook and the other as the Floor Runner/Intake Screener.
- **Establish Clear Callouts**: Use concise codes (e.g., *"Door 2 breach," "Pantry clear"*) rather than vague statements.
- **Double-Check Critical Doors**: Have the runner visually confirm that manual locks engaged after the monitor hits facility switches.

Consistently checking off each item on your list of **animal daycare anomaly objectives** ensures that zero entities breach your perimeter while keeping the children comfortable and secure until your shift ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I fail one of the animal daycare anomaly objectives during a shift?
Failing minor caretaking chores (like missing a meal) lowers your shift evaluation score and raises panic levels among the kids. However, failing security-focused **animal daycare anomaly objectives**—such as accidentally admitting an impostor or leaving security shutters unlatched—triggers immediate hostile encounters that can result in a game-over screen.

### How can I distinguish subtle anomalies from regular visitors?
Always compare physical characteristics directly against the daily reference manual. Anomalies frequently exhibit tiny flaws, such as asymmetrical pupils, six-fingered hands, reversed ID credentials, or voices with an unnatural metallic reverb. Take an extra two seconds to examine shadows before unlocking entrance doors.

### Can all nighttime events be prevented?
While you cannot stop scheduled nocturnal events from starting, you can neutralize their danger. Securing window locks, maintaining battery levels in emergency flashlights, and performing regular hallway sweeps prevent anomalies from breaching the main nursery area.

### What is the most important control to master when completing animal daycare anomaly objectives?
The interaction key (`E`) combined with fast surveillance access (`F` or `Spacebar`) are the most critical inputs. Fast cycling between physical defense actions and terminal surveillance enables you to catch perimeter threats early without neglecting floor duties.
