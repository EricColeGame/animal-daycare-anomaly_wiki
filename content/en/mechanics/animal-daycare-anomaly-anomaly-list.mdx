## Checkpoint Inspection Protocols: How to Spot Fakes

Systematic inspections minimize slip-ups when queues stack up outside. Taking shortcuts during intake invites disaster. Community reports suggest following a top-to-bottom bodily scan every single time a visitor steps up to the glass.

Use this standardized evaluation checklist during intake:

1. **Verify Eye Symmetry:** Authentic kids blink at natural intervals (every 4–7 seconds). Disguised visitors often stare indefinitely or exhibit uncoordinated eye movements.
2. **Review Limb Count:** Count fingers, toes, ears, and tail joints. Many biological anomalies generate redundant extremities.
3. **Conduct Audio Verification:** When asking standard intake questions, listen closely to speech cadence. Synthesized, pitched-down, or overlapping voices signal a mimic.
4. **Mirror Double-Check:** Glancing at the reflection behind the counter confirms the creature casts a physical reflection with the correct silhouette.

## Common Inspection Mistakes to Avoid

Even veteran players get caught off guard during chaotic double shifts. Community reports highlight several repeated blunders:
