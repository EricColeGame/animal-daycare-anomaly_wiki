## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most common signs of an anomaly at intake?
The most reliable indicators include abnormal physical geometry (such as inverted joints or extra fingers), spelling discrepancies on nametags, flickering or missing shadows under overhead lighting, and unnatural blink frequencies. Always compare newcomers directly to your reference manual before unlocking the main lobby doors.

### What should I do if an anomaly bypasses the front desk?
If an unverified entity enters the daycare, sound the silent alarm immediately to herd verified children into safe rooms. Pull up the security monitors to pinpoint the anomaly's current sector, seal the corresponding bulkheads, and prepare non-lethal deterrent tools or call containment teams depending on your active shift phase.

### How do child happiness levels affect anomaly detection rules?
When real children become hungry, frightened, or bored, their crying creates loud background noise that drowns out essential audio cues like vent crawling, floorboard creaks, and distorted breathing. Keeping children calm is vital to hearing subtle audio anomalies in nearby rooms.

### Do the animal daycare anomaly rules change when playing in multiplayer?
While the fundamental detection criteria remain identical across both modes, multiplayer introduces divided responsibilities and higher entity spawn rates. Teams must cross-reference their intake logs and camera feeds to ensure that no player accidentally admits a flagged impostor while others are busy with meal preparation or maintenance tasks.
