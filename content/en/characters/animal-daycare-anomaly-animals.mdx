### Physical Telltales and Genetic Glitches

The entities attempting to breach the nursery are rarely perfect copies. They rely on caretakers being too distracted by mini-games and chores to notice small anatomical discrepancies. Always perform a top-to-bottom scan:

*   **Eyes and Pupils:** Look for mismatched colors, horizontal goat-like slits on carnivores, or a complete lack of a blinking animation.
*   **Limbs and Digits:** Count fingers, toes, and claws. Many anomalous variants struggle to render the correct number of extremities.
*   **Facial Geometry:** Disproportionate snouts, upside-down smiles, or facial textures that appear stretched across the model indicate an active shapeshifter.
*   **Shadow Anomalies:** A visitor standing directly under an overhead bulb without casting a shadow—or casting a shadow with a distinct silhouette—must be rejected on the spot.

To consistently identify impostors among genuine **animal daycare anomaly animals**, you must use every tool in your security booth. Relying solely on direct line of sight through the reception window is a recipe for failure.

### How to Execute a Flawless Inspection

1.  **Halt the Visitor at the Perimeter Gate:** Never open the inner airlock until initial visual verification is complete.
2.  **Request Identification and Medical Forms:** Check for typos, smudged ink, or mismatched emergency contact numbers.
3.  **Perform the Three-Point Anatomy Scan:** Systematically sweep eyes, hands/paws, and silhouette.
4.  **Engage the Audio Intercom:** Ask standard greeting prompts. Anomaly entities often stutter, replicate pre-recorded child voices with metallic echo, or fail to respond entirely.
5.  **Corroborate with CCTV:** Toggle your security monitor to view the arrival from another angle. Some entities only display their true, horrifying silhouette when viewed through electronic lens sensors.

Player experience shows that keeping a notepad to track admitted names and species prevents confusion when duplicate visitors attempt to claim the same bunk during evening headcount.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if an animal in the daycare is an anomaly?
Look for anatomical inconsistencies such as mismatched eyes, extra digits, unnatural joints, or missing shadows. Anomalies also exhibit strange behavior during mini-games, refuse standard meals, or produce distorted audio through the intercom.

### What happens if I accidentally admit animal daycare anomaly animals?
Admitting an impostor triggers aggressive nighttime events. Once the lights go out, the disguised anomaly will shed its disguise, wander the facility, terrorize genuine children, and hunt the staff. You must use security doors, lights, and distractions to survive until 6:00 AM.

### Can real children become anomalies during the shift?
Based on community reports, genuine children do not transform spontaneously. However, lurking entities can kidnap real children and take their place if rooms are left unmonitored or unlit during afternoon activities. Always keep play areas well-lit and accounted for.

### What is the most reliable tool for spotting disguised animal daycare anomaly animals?
The security CCTV monitor combined with the infrared heat scanner is the most dependable setup. While anomalies can visually mimic fur and clothing, they frequently fail to reproduce natural mammalian body heat and digital camera rendering tricks.
