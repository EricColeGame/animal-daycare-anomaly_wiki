## Nighttime Survival: Managing Hostile Entities

Once the sun sets, the operational focus changes from routine caregiving to active defense. If any disguised **animal daycare anomaly characters** slipped past your daytime screening, they reveal their true forms during the night shift. Surviving the dark requires tight security camera monitoring, strategic circuit management, and rapid lockdown responses.

### Critical Nighttime Defense Procedures

During the night phase, hostiles wander the hallways attempting to reach the sleeping quarters. Your job is to keep real kids calm while rerouting or repelling wandering entities.

1.  **Monitor the Hallway Cameras:** Check high-traffic corridors every 10 to 15 seconds. Note any shadow that breaks line-of-sight sensors.
2.  **Maintain Auxiliary Power:** Lights keep many hostile types at bay, but running the breakers constantly trips the power grid. Cycle illumination zone by zone.
3.  **Deploy Sound Distractions:** If an anomaly approaches the nursery doors, activate remote toy stations down the hall to lure it away.
4.  **Administer Sedatives/Soothers:** If real children wake up crying from anomalous noises, soothe them immediately. High noise levels draw nighttime stalkers directly to the dormitories.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I accidentally admit an anomaly into the daycare?
Admitting an impostor does not cause an immediate loss, but it creates a dangerous hazard for the night phase. The creature will wander among the genuine children, steadily raising their terror levels. During the dark hours, it will shed its disguise and actively hunt both players and genuine visitors, forcing you to track it down or lock it into an isolated sector before it breaches the dormitory.

### How can I tell if a child's crying sound is real or an anomaly trap?
Always verify audio cues using your security tablet before running to investigate. Genuine children produce steady, localized crying sounds from designated sleeping mats. Mimic anomalies often produce audio that loops unnaturally, carries subtle background static, or emanates from unpopulated areas like supply closets, maintenance shafts, or closed exterior hallways.

### Do animal daycare anomaly characters change their appearance across different runs?
Yes. Procedural randomization applies to both legitimate kids and anomalous entities each shift. An animal model that was completely safe in your first round might appear in your next shift carrying an anomaly trait, such as an extra joint or incorrect iris shape. You must re-inspect every visitor on every shift without relying on assumptions from previous runs.

### What is the most reliable tool for catching high-threat mimics?
The UV inspection lamp combined with the intake desk mirror provides the highest detection accuracy. While standard physical textures can look convincing under dim ambient lighting, UV illumination highlights skin seams, unnatural discoloration, and disguised features that standard optical cameras miss completely.
