### Environmental and Structural Anomalies

These anomalies do not hunt you directly; instead, they destabilize the daycare's safety rating and induce panic in genuine children. Typical signs include:

1. **Geometry Warping:** Doors appearing on walls where windows used to be.
2. **Audio Echoes:** Disembodied footsteps sounding from empty corridors.
3. **Decal Inversion:** Posters and rules on the wall hanging upside down or displaying corrupted imagery.

### Entity and Impostor Anomalies

Entity-based anomalies are dangerous impostors posing as children or staff. Identifying their entry points among known **animal daycare anomaly locations** prevents lethal nighttime encounters.

* **The Mimic Child:** Looks almost identical to real visitors, but fails basic behavioral mini-games or refuses food.
* **The Shadow Lurker:** Clings to high corners near ceiling lights in the hallway, only visible through the infrared feed.
* **The Wandering Janitor:** An autonomous figure roaming corridors after lockdown who does not appear on official shift schedules.

## Expert Tips for Spotting Subtle Daycare Anomalies

Mastering the mechanics requires going beyond obvious visual bugs. Experienced players utilize specific audio and camera tricks to isolate discrepancies before they escalate.

* **Listen for Irregular Breathing:** In the quiet nap room, every real child has a synchronized breathing sound file. An unsynced or rapid breath pattern indicates an impostor cot.
* **Verify Reflections:** Mirrors in the washroom and glass partitions in reception frequently fail to render impostors correctly. If a visitor stands before a mirror and casts no reflection, isolate them immediately.
* **Watch Light Sources:** When passing through hallway **animal daycare anomaly locations**, your flashlight beam should remain steady. An erratic stutter or color shift from white to pale green warns that a hostile presence is sharing the corridor.
* **Monitor the Sanity Bar:** If your character begins swaying or breathing heavily while inspecting a room, an anomaly is present nearby, even if it is currently hidden behind furniture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most dangerous animal daycare anomaly locations during night shifts?
The nap room and the central hallway are the most dangerous locations. Because lighting is drastically reduced during the night cycle, impostor entities frequently replace sleeping animals or stalk the shadows just outside your monitoring office.

### How do I tell a real animal child from an impostor anomaly?
Inspect every visitor at the reception desk by checking their species traits against their admission badges. Impostors typically display subtle flaws, such as mismatched eyes, extra digits on their paws, reversed markings, or unnatural dialogue responses when questioned.

### Do animal daycare anomaly locations change with every playthrough?
Yes. While the daycare building layout remains constant, anomaly spawns are procedural. A room that was completely clear during your previous shift may feature multiple environmental or biological anomalies in your next run.

### What should I do immediately after finding an anomaly?
Log the discrepancy using your security console or room terminal. If the anomaly is an aggressive entity, seal the corresponding security partition and use the designated deterrent, such as ceiling lights or intercom alarms, to neutralize it before continuing your inspection rounds.
