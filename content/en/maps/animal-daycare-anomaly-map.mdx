## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most dangerous room on the animal daycare anomaly map?
Community players widely consider the Dormitory to be the most dangerous zone during the night phase. Low lighting, multiple blind spots underneath bunks, and proximity to maintenance vents make it an easy target for infiltrations. During the day, the Intake Vestibule carries the highest risk if you approve an entry without double-checking clues.

### Where can I find the fuse box when the lights go out?
The fuse box is located in the Utility Wing, through the unmarked metal door just past the Kitchen hallway on the eastern edge of the **animal daycare anomaly map**. Always carry a flashlight when heading to this area, as anomalies often camp utility corridors during power outages.

### How many cameras are featured on the animal daycare anomaly map?
The standard security console features six active camera channels covering the Front Gate, Intake Lobby, Playroom North, Atrium Hub, Dining Hall, and Dormitory Center. There are no cameras inside the walk-in pantry or inside the utility closets.

### Can anomalies climb through the daycare vents?
Yes. Several anomalies can access the ventilation shaft connecting the Kitchen pantry, the Utility Wing, and the Playroom ceiling. If you hear metal rattling overhead while moving across the **animal daycare anomaly map**, leave the immediate area and verify room feeds on the security desk monitors.
